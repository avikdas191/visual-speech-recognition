Session-based split for rebuilt dataset
=======================================

Source: 60 recording sessions, 10 words each (600 videos)
Split: 42 train / 9 validation / 9 test sessions

Method: stratified random assignment by recording location,
using random.Random(seed).shuffle within each location group.

Seed: 4

Seed selection: seeds were checked against a fixed criterion set
before searching - that all five head angles (normal, up, left,
right, down) appear in both the test and validation splits.
Angle was not stratified directly because 9 test sessions across
5 angles and 8 locations gives strata too small to fill.
Seeds 42, 7, 3, 11, 5, 13, 17 and 4 were examined; seed 4 gave
the most even angle distribution meeting the criterion.
The criterion concerns condition coverage only, not model accuracy.

Rule: augmented images are generated only from sessions within
their own split. No augmented image derives from a session
assigned to a different split.
