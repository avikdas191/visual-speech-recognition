Dataset diagnostics
===================

Checks run on the rebuilt dataset before architecture work began.
No training involved; these are measurements of the data itself.


FRAME PADDING
-------------
Videos shorter than 60 frames are extended by repeating the final
frame. Extent across the 600 source videos:

  padded at all:        123
  padded >  5 frames:    46
  padded > 10 frames:    15
  padded > 15 frames:     4

So roughly 92% of videos are padded by 5 frames or fewer, which is
negligible. The problem is concentrated rather than widespread.

Worst affected sessions, by total padded frames across their 10 videos:

  set_28    102 frames    worst single video: 20
  set_20     97                               20
  set_19     53                               15
  set_34     51                               14

The extreme cases are set_28/bat and set_20/eat, both from 40-frame
source videos. In those grids, 20 of the 60 cells are the same
repeated still image - a third of the input is one frozen frame, and
whatever articulation occurred after frame 40 was never recorded.


WHERE THE AFFECTED SESSIONS LANDED
----------------------------------
  set_19  train
  set_20  train
  set_28  train
  set_34  TEST

Three of the four worst sessions are in training, where the effect is
dilution across 42 sessions and is tolerable.

set_34 is in the test split. With only 9 test sessions, this is 11% of
the primary measurement carrying moderately degraded input, and it may
slightly depress the reported accuracy.

The split was NOT redrawn in response to this. The assignment was made
by a documented rule with a fixed seed before any data-quality
inspection, and moving a session out of test because it looks
unfavourable would be selection after the fact. The figure stands as
measured, with this characteristic recorded.

Follow-up available: once a better model exists, compare per-session
accuracy across the 9 test sessions. If set_34 scores markedly below
the other eight, that supports padding as a contributing factor. If
not, the concern was overstated. Either way it is an observation drawn
from the data rather than an adjustment made to it.


CROP SCALE - EXAMINED AND SET ASIDE
-----------------------------------
The cropping code handles two cases differently. When the mouth
bounding box is smaller than 112x80, padding is added and the mouth
keeps its natural size. When the box is larger, no padding is added
and the crop is resized down, so the mouth fills the frame.

This raised a concern that apparent mouth scale might vary with lip
opening, since open-mouth frames produce larger bounding boxes.

Visual inspection of cropped output across sets 5, 7, 9, 45 and 50
found consistent framing with no visible scale variation between
open and closed positions. The likely explanation is that a 112x80
window is generous relative to a mouth at this recording framing, so
the resize path rarely triggers.

Recorded as examined and not pursued. If certainty is wanted later,
logging the raw bounding box dimensions during cropping would settle
it directly.


AUGMENTATION VOLUME - DEFERRED
------------------------------
Whether 70 augmented sets is the right number cannot be tested against
the current model. That model reaches 1.0000 training accuracy by
epoch 9 with roughly 57,000 parameters per training sample - it
memorises whatever it is given. Adding data would produce no
measurable change, and the correct conclusion would be that the model,
not the data volume, is the constraint.

The question becomes answerable once parameter count is brought in
line with dataset size. Deferred until after architecture work.
